# What Northstar's members were told

*Fictional. This is the note the demo community's coach posted to members before turning the loop on. Every owner posts their own version, in their own words. The loop doesn't run until members have been told.*

---

**How I keep in touch with you**

Hi everyone,

With this many of us, I can't always notice when someone's gone quiet or hasn't found their footing yet. So I've started using an AI assistant called Fo to help me keep track.

Here's how it works:

- Fo looks at activity in our member list (when you last joined in, your goals, your wins) and suggests who I should check in with.
- Fo drafts a short note. I read every one and decide whether it goes out. **Every message you get is from me, and I approve each one.**
- Sometimes I'll add a short voice note. You'll hear Fo, my assistant, passing on my words, never a copy of my voice.
- Nothing ever happens inside the community itself. Messages come from my own email.

If you'd rather not get these check-ins, just reply "no check-ins" to any of them, or tell me. I'll take you off, no questions asked.

Alex
