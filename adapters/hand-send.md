# Adapter: hand-send

The fallback for every platform, and the right choice when automated sending isn't allowed or isn't set up.

- Fo puts the approved text and the member's profile link (or address) in the outreach doc under the draft, marked **Ready to send by hand**, and lists them in the morning summary.
- The owner sends it and replies "sent" on the call or writes "sent" under "Your decision" in the doc.
- Fo then sets the draft to `Sent`, adds the Touchpoints row and updates `last_contacted`.
